# EduAI — Feature One-Pager

_An AI-powered school platform: one login for the principal, the staff room, the lab and every student._

---

## ⭐ Highlight Features

**1. AI Tutor**
A subject teacher available to every student at any hour, in plain language.
Works by reading the school's own uploaded textbook chapters and answering only from them.
Result: on-syllabus answers, no wrong or off-topic information reaching a child.

**2. AI Question Paper Generator**
A teacher picks class, subject and chapter and gets a full question paper in seconds.
Works by pulling the actual chapter text and generating questions from it, showing the source page beside each one.
Result: papers a teacher can verify at a glance — hours of work reduced to minutes.

**3. Virtual Science Labs**
Chemistry, Physics and Biology practicals that run on screen — no equipment needed.
Works as built-in simulations: periodic table and atomic models, projectile motion, friction, sound waves, plant and animal cells.
Result: schools without lab infrastructure can still teach practical science.

**4. Learning Games**
17 original games that turn practice into something children choose to do.
Works by mapping each game to a real NCERT chapter — fractions, number lines, area, division, crosswords, word building, grammar, story order.
Result: daily practice with measurable progress, not screen time.

**5. AI-Assisted Grading with Teacher Approval**
Answer papers are evaluated automatically, then held for the teacher.
Works by scoring each response and presenting it to the teacher, who edits and approves before results are released.
Result: fast turnaround with a human always accountable for the final mark.

---

## Core Platform

**6. Complete Exam Lifecycle**
Create, schedule, conduct and release exams from one screen.
Works with a reusable question bank, a different question order for every student, and controlled result release.
Result: fair online exams with no leakage and no manual paper handling.

**7. Reports & Analytics**
Class performance, subject-wise weak areas and individual progress, live.
Works by tracking every exam, task and game attempt and rolling it up for teacher, admin and principal views.
Result: decisions based on evidence instead of impressions.

**8. School Setup & Student Management**
Classes, sections, subjects, teachers, lab incharges and students in one place.
Works with spreadsheet bulk import, plus proper handling of transfers, exits and archiving.
Result: an entire school onboarded in a day, records never lost.

**9. Year-End Promotion**
Move the whole school into the next academic year in one action.
Works as a single controlled, repeat-safe operation across every class and section.
Result: what used to take weeks of clerical work becomes one click.

**10. Timetable, Live Sessions & Tasks**
Day-to-day teaching operations, coordinated.
Works through a timetable builder (or import), live class sessions with attendance, and task assignment to a class or a single student.
Result: teachers and students always know what is happening next.

**11. School Content Library & Branding**
The school's own books, materials, name, logo and colours.
Works by letting admins upload approved content that the AI then teaches from, under the school's own identity.
Result: the platform feels like the school's, and teaches what the school approves.

**12. Central Control & Feature Packages**
Every school gets exactly the features it paid for.
Works through remote per-school switches for AI Tutor, Exam Generator, Labs, Games, Leaderboards, Previous-Year Papers, Reports and Content Upload — plus a full audit log and support desk.
Result: flexible pricing and complete oversight, with no code change per customer.

---

**What makes it defensible:** the AI teaches only from the school's own books with the source shown; nothing reaches a student without teacher approval; all 17 labs and games are built and owned in-house; and the AI engine can be swapped or moved to our own servers without rebuilding the product.
