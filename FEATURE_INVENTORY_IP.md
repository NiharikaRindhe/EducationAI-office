# EduAI — Feature List

_18 August 2026_

An AI-powered school platform. One login for the whole school — the principal's
office, the staff room, the lab, and every student — with age-appropriate
experiences for primary, middle and senior classes.

---

## For Students

**AI Tutor**
A subject teacher available at any hour. Students ask questions in plain language
and get answers drawn from their own textbook chapters — not generic internet
answers. The tutor stays on syllabus and refuses to wander off-subject.

**Virtual Science Labs**
Chemistry, Physics and Biology experiments that run on screen — periodic table
and atomic models, projectile motion and friction, sound waves, interactive plant
and animal cells. Schools without lab equipment can still teach practicals.

**Learning Games**
17 built-in games mapped to actual NCERT chapters — fractions, number lines, area
and division, crosswords, word building, grammar, story sequencing and more.
Practice that children choose to do.

**Exams & Practice**
Online tests, previous-year question papers, instant results, and detailed
answer review so students see where they went wrong.

**Daily Motivation**
Daily challenges, points, badges, streaks and class leaderboards.

**Study Tools**
Personal notes, concept maps, board-exam preparation, focus timer, assigned tasks
and a personal progress dashboard.

## For Teachers

**AI Question Paper Generator**
Pick a class, subject and chapter — get a full question paper in seconds. Every
question is generated from the school's own uploaded textbook, with the page it
came from shown alongside, so the teacher can verify before printing.

**Question Bank**
A growing, reusable library of questions per class and subject.

**Exams, Start to Finish**
Create, schedule and release exams. Each student gets the questions in a
different order. AI assists with grading; the teacher reviews and approves before
any result is published.

**Assign & Track**
Assign tasks and homework to a class or an individual, and see who has done what.

**Live Sessions**
Run a live class session with attendance.

**Reports**
Class performance, subject-wise weak areas, and individual student progress.

**Timetable**
Personal teaching timetable, always current.

## For School Admins

**Full School Setup**
Classes, sections, subjects, teachers, lab incharges and students — all managed
from one place.

**Bulk Student Management**
Import an entire school roll from a spreadsheet. Handle transfers, exits and
archiving without losing records.

**Year-End Promotion**
Promote the whole school to the next academic year in one controlled action.

**Timetable Builder**
Build the school timetable, or import an existing one.

**Content Library**
Upload the school's own textbooks and materials — everything the AI teaches from
is then the school's own approved content.

**School Branding**
The platform carries the school's name, logo and colours.

**Feature Control**
Turn features on or off for the school.

**Principal's Report**
A single school-wide view of usage, performance and progress.

**Support Desk**
Raise and track issues; staff and students can raise tickets too.

**Admit Cards & Slips**
Generate and print exam admit cards and slips.

## For Lab Incharges

A dedicated login to manage lab sessions, and to see which students and teachers
are using the labs.

## For the Platform Owner (Super Admin)

**School Onboarding** — bring a new school live in a guided flow.

**Per-School Packages** — sell features individually: AI Tutor, AI Exam Generator,
Virtual Labs, Games, Leaderboards, Previous-Year Papers, Reports, Content Upload.
Each school gets exactly what it paid for, switched on or off remotely.

**Central Content Portal** — curriculum content published once, available to every
school.

**AI Console** — control which AI models power each feature, and monitor usage and
cost per school.

**Audit Log** — a complete, exportable record of who did what, with filters.

**Directory & Oversight** — cross-school student directory with privacy controls,
plus a support inbox for every school.

## Website (Public)

Landing page, features page, pricing page, and school sign-in.

---

## What Makes It Different

1. **The AI teaches from the school's own books.** Answers and question papers come
   from the actual chapters uploaded by the school, with the source page shown —
   not from an AI's general knowledge.
2. **Nothing reaches a student unchecked.** Generated questions and AI grading are
   reviewed and approved by a teacher first.
3. **Labs and games are built in-house.** 17 original interactive experiences, owned
   outright — not licensed or embedded from anyone else.
4. **Sold feature by feature.** Every school can be given a different package,
   controlled centrally, with no code changes.
5. **Not tied to any AI company.** The AI engine can be swapped, or moved onto our
   own servers, without rebuilding the product.
